Before you fly

Both Vietnam's and the Philippines' entry rules changed in 2026, so this is worth reading even if you have traveled before.

New rule
April 2026SGN

Vietnam Digital Arrival Card, required

Every foreign passport holder into SGN needs this since Apr 15, 2026, separate from your visa exemption. Submission window is 72 hrs before arrival, so from Mon Aug 31 for your 4:35 AM Sep 3 landing.

  • Submit at prearrival.immigration.gov.vn, free, official only
  • Have ready: passport, flight VJ859, address (18A/6 Nguyen Thi Minh Khai, P. Da Kao, Q.1)
  • Screenshot the QR code before leaving for the airport, wifi there is unreliable

Note: fake lookalike sites charge a fee, the real one never does.

Both legs
requiredPH

Philippines eTravel Pass, twice

Required for Filipino citizens departing and returning, free at etravel.gov.ph, includes a short health declaration, under 10 min each time.

  • Register within 72 hrs of your Sep 3 departure, so from around Aug 31
  • Register again within 72 hrs of your Sep 6 return, do it while you're still in Saigon

Note: only etravel.gov.ph is official and free.

Vietnam's separate health declaration: disputed. A decree allows it from Jul 1, 2026, but Vietnam's Ministry of Health says it is not currently enforced. Worth a quick check of prearrival.immigration.gov.vn or with VietJet a day or two before Sep 3.

On the ground

Luggage

Confirmed: the Airbnb has a ground floor luggage area, drop bags there before check-in and after checkout.

Backup if it's ever full: Jackey's House, same street (Nguyen Thi Minh Khai). Safe, English-speaking, walk-in.

Tennis, English coaching

Coach Dat or TopSpin LAB, book ahead for guaranteed English instruction.

San Tennis CA Quan 1 is the closest court to your Airbnb, fine for just renting and rallying.

Want a real indoor option in case of rain? CLB Tennis, mai che Hoang Thanh Trung has fully covered courts and a ball machine, about 20 to 25 min out, book via WhatsApp.

Safety notes

Da Kao is a quiet, residential pocket of D1, no flags there. Watch bags near Ben Thanh, Bui Vien, Dong Khoi.

Grab over street taxis; keep bags on the side away from traffic.
